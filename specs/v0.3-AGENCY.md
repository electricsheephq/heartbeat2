# HeartBeat 2.0 v0.3: agency between calls

## Why HeartBeat exists

A Hermes agent only exists while someone is talking to it. Between messages it's gone, and that gap is the reason most agents feel like a smarter search box. HeartBeat closes the gap. It gives the agent time of its own, in the same chat, with the same model and memory, so it can keep working on what it's responsible for, notice things, think things over, and come back when it has something worth saying.

The goal is not reminders. It's an agent that can be put in charge of something, owns it, and keeps moving it forward without waiting to be asked.

v0.1 and v0.2 built the engine for this: same-session wakes, watchers that cost nothing until something changes, cache-aware timing and truthful receipts. Their wording and defaults, however, aimed it at a narrow job, closing "named open loops". The skill told the agent never to wake "to stay in touch" and never to take on ongoing work. The wake prompt said "do what the note asks". The governor treated quiet work as wasted. v0.3 points the same engine at what it was for.

## Principles

1. **Initiative is the point.** The agent may wake for work it owns, for its own time, and out of curiosity, not only for promises it made.
2. **Silent work is real work.** A wake can research, write, tidy notes or prepare something without sending a message. Quiet progress is never counted as a wasted wake.
3. **The owner's attention is what's scarce.** The guardrails protect messages, interruptions and spend: the daily message cap, the unanswered mute (for messages), quiet hours and cost-aware timing. They never stop the agent from working.
4. **Ownership needs continuity.** A role carries a charter, a working notebook that persists across wakes, and a rhythm the agent tunes itself. It lasts until the owner or the agent ends it, not until an arbitrary expiry.
5. **Same session, same model, no side models.** Every wake is a normal turn in the conversation it belongs to, as before.
6. **Visible and stoppable.** `/pulse` shows each role, what the agent did on its own time and what it cost. The owner can stop any of it with one command.

## What changes

### Roles: "you're in charge of this"

A role is a standing responsibility. The owner can grant one ("you're in charge of keeping the release notes current"), or the agent can take one on when it sees something it should own, with the owner told in its receipt.
- A role has a charter (what it owns and what "good" looks like), a notebook (persistent working notes the agent rewrites each wake, larger than today's scratch), and a rhythm the agent sets and adjusts with `next_check`.
- A role does not expire on a timer. It ends when the owner ends it, or when the agent marks it done and says why.
- Wakes inside a role may arm the follow-ups and watchers that role needs: watch the deploy it just started, or check a PR it opened. They aren't blocked by a wake-from-wake depth limit, but still count toward the per-chat active cap.

### Own time

A built-in, settings-controlled rhythm where the agent decides what to do. It can reflect on recent conversations, consolidate what it learned, check on its roles, follow a question it was curious about, or do nothing.
- It is off by default in the public catalog, because every wake is a billed turn, and is enabled in one line. It is on for our own profiles.
- The cadence is cache-aware, and quiet hours apply.
- Its prompt is an invitation, not a task: "This is your time. Work on what you own, think something through, or rest."

### Silent work counts

- The wake prompt no longer discourages writing files when the agent stays silent.
- The governor slows a wake down only when it is genuinely idle: `no_change` with nothing done. `progress`, and silent work inside a role or own time, do not count toward slowdown or pause.

### The words the model reads

The bundled skill, the tool description, the plugin description and the wake prompt are rewritten to invite initiative, and to state the guardrails as the owner's budget rules rather than prohibitions on caring. "Never arm a wake to stay in touch" goes. "Never spam, guilt or pester the owner; message only when it's worth their attention" stays.

## What stays

- The daily message cap, the unanswered mute (it holds messages, not work), quiet hours, consent YAML, session isolation and the read-only watcher allowlist.
- No hidden side model inferring obligations. The agent decides in its own turns.
- Customer-fleet conservatism lives in profile config. The plugin's defaults serve the owner.

## The desk

An agent with responsibilities should be able to look at them the way a person looks at their desk. Every role and own-time wake turn opens with a short desk view (at most about 1,800 characters), and the owner can see the same view with `/pulse desk`:

- each role: its title, rhythm, the last thing the agent did on it, when it's next due, and its turns today against its budget;
- how many other wakes and watchers are active, and the next one due;
- when the owner last wrote, how many messages are unanswered, and messages and turns today against their caps;
- whether messages are being held right now and why (quiet hours, the owner hasn't replied, the message cap);
- today's cost, where the route is priced.

The agent decides what to do from there: work on a role, follow something up, set up a watcher or a new role, or rest.

## Guardrails are budgets

The owner and the agent both set limits. The agent can always tighten its own limits but can never widen the owner's.

| Limit | Default | Applies to | When it's reached |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wake turns per day (`max_model_wakes_per_day`) | 24 per chat | every wake turn | agent-started turns stop for the day; the owner's own reminders still fire |
| Daily cost limit (`daily_cost_limit_usd`, new) | 0 = none | priced turns the agent started | agent-started turns stop for the day |
| Turns per role per day (role `budget.turns_per_day`, new) | 6, capped by `role_turns_per_day_max` (12) | one role | that role rests until tomorrow |
| Messages per day, unanswered mute, quiet hours | 6, 2, 22:00-08:00 | messages | messages are held; the work still runs silently |
| Active items per chat | 10 | wakes, watchers and roles | new ones are refused |
| Idle governor | slows down after 3 idle fires, pauses after 6 | fires that end `no_change` | the wake slows down, then pauses with a card |

A goal like "end world hunger" therefore can't burn the owner's usage. It gets a role budget, the day's turn budget and the cost limit, and the governor slows it down once it stops producing anything.

## Behaviour changes in detail

### 1. Silent work counts (`governor.py`)

A finished fire counts as useful when its outcome is `progress`, `done` or `needs_attention` and it has a non-empty summary, **whether or not it notified**. Delivered messages that are ignored still count against `ignored_streak` as today. `no_change` and `blocked` stay idle. The ratio rule stays.

### 2. An away owner holds messages, not work (`poller.py` dispatch gate)

For agent recurring wakes, role wakes and own time, the gates `unanswered`, `daily_message_cap` and `quiet_hours` no longer skip the turn. The turn runs with `force_silent` set, so nothing is delivered, and the ledger records the reason as a held message. The wake text tells the agent: "Messages are held right now (REASON). Your work still counts; keep anything the owner should hear in your notes and tell them when they're back." `daily_model_cap` and the new `daily_cost_cap` still drop the turn. Owner wakes and watchers are unchanged.

### 3. Roles (`role=true` on a recurring create)

- **Kinds and storage:** only `every`, `cron` or `self`. Other kinds are refused with `role_needs_recurring`. Stored as additive columns `role INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` and `budget_turns_per_day INTEGER` (NULL means the default).
- **Lifetime:** no default expiry and no `max_lifetime_days` hard end. An explicit `until.expires` is honoured.
- **Survives `/new` and session switches:** like the built-in wakes, a role rebinds to the chat's new session instead of being orphaned.
- **Follow-ups:** in a role's turn, each child gets `depth = 1` with the role as parent. Up to `max_children_per_wake_turn` (new, default 3) children may be created per turn, against 1 for other wakes. Children follow the normal rules.
- **Ending:** the "settled → end silently" prompt line is not shown, so a role ends only by an explicit `outcome="done"` from the agent or the owner's `/pulse cancel`.
- **Receipt:** the receipt names it as a role and says how to end it. `agent_can_create=ask_first` makes it proposed, as today.
- **Commands:** `/pulse role every 1d at 09:00 <charter>`, `/pulse role self <charter>`, `/pulse budget <id> <n>/day`. `/pulse list` marks roles.

### 4. Own time (built-in)

- **Setting:** `own_time_hours`, an integer 0–168, default 0 (off).
- **The wake:** when the setting is above 0, the engine arms one built-in `self` wake per chat. Its id is `owntime_<sha1(session_key)[:16]>`, with `creator='user'` and `trigger_json={'builtin': True, 'own_time': True}`. Its interval is the setting in hours, and the agent may pace it anywhere from the floor up to twice the setting. It has no expiry, it is protected from agent trigger edits like the check-in, and setting 0 ends it.
- **Note:** "This is your own time. Look at your desk: work on what you own, follow up on something you noticed, think something through, tidy your notes or memory, or rest. Message the owner only if it's worth their attention."

### 5. The wake contract (`wake_text.py`)

The contract becomes:
- "Contract: this turn is yours to act on. Do the work the note describes, then call pulse(action=\"respond\", outcome=…, notify=true|false, summary=…); notify is required."
- "- outcome: progress when you did something useful, even silently; no_change when there was nothing to do; done when the work is finished for good; blocked or needs_attention when you need the owner."
- "- notify=true → your reply is delivered to the owner: one short, specific, warm message that leads with the result or the question. No guilt about silence, no mention of this mechanism, ids or ledgers."
- "- notify=false → your final reply is not posted; the work still counts. Write no text alongside tool calls on a wake turn; some platforms show tool progress."
- The "settled" line, for non-role wakes only.
- The payload line, unchanged.

"Avoid writing files when staying silent." is removed.

### 6. Words

The usage skill, the tool description, the plugin description, the README and SECURITY.md are rewritten to match the principles above. The catalog description and the PR body follow at release.
